# Videre 1.22.38 Beta 2

Music now has its own home experience, with a visible Videos / Music switch in the top bar. This is an opt-in prerelease; the latest stable release remains v1.22.37.

## Music home and navigation

- **Home** shows public new releases, global chart playlists, and mood/genre shortcuts. **Explore** provides release grids, charts by country, and curated mood/genre playlists. These feeds work without a Google login and do not present account-based recommendations or synchronized likes.
- **Library** keeps saved songs, albums, artists, and public playlists in your Videre profile, alongside local Music playlists and audio downloads. Bookmarks and preferences are included in library backups; downloaded files remain on the device.
- Phone Music pages support swipes and visible tabs. Tablets use a navigation rail; TVs have remote-accessible Music destinations and search. Switching modes preserves each mode's navigation and keeps the shared phone/tablet player running.
- Choose **Settings → Browsing → Home experience**, or use home/navigation customization, for **Videos and Music**, **Videos only**, or **Music only**. Single-mode choices hide the switch and open the selected experience directly. TV exposes the same choice in Settings. Preferences belong to each profile.
- Existing video navigation customization is preserved. The old Music shortcut is replaced by the top-bar switch without resetting other saved shortcuts.

## Regression fixes

- Back from Music mood details, local playlists, and directly opened catalog pages returns to the correct page. Hidden Video tabs no longer consume Music Back presses or change the saved Video tab.
- Hidden pages do not receive keyboard/remote focus. Profile and session changes retire old pages, responses, and open mode-picker choices.
- Home customization remains usable on short tablet displays. Video cards reserve room for enlarged text without stretching their compact artwork.
- Long translated mode labels remain inside the top bar at larger text sizes, with their full accessibility labels and tooltips retained.
- Music song actions track saves made from other screens and honor the displayed Save/Remove action and the song the menu was opened for, even if playback advances. Merging a backup combines saved Music items and local-playlist markers, while preserving existing items and checking combined storage limits atomically.
- Music retains the existing Invidious playback, download, and SponsorBlock behavior. Paid sponsorship and non-music sections remain enabled by default unless explicitly disabled; skipping depends on available community data. New downloads cache available segments for offline playback.

## Installation and verification

Install **app-release.apk** for the universal build, or choose the APK matching your device. This beta uses Videre's existing package and signing identity, with a higher version code than Beta 1. Export a library backup before trying a beta; returning to an older version may require reinstalling.

Validation includes repository formatting/static analysis, offline regressions, live anonymous Music checks, Android builds, and native emulator acceptance for phone, tablet dimensions, and the TV interface. Online and offline Music playback, cached segment skipping, ordinary video playback, navigation retention, and single-mode settings were exercised. Physical Android devices/TVs and the full nix-shell live Invidious suite were not tested. Google-account synchronization and gapless playback remain outside this release.

The release workflow verifies APK version numbers and the existing signing certificate. `SHA256SUMS` covers the attached APKs, bundles, and source archives.
