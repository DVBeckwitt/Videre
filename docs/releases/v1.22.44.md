# Videre 1.22.44

- Music Home puts saved songs first and suggests searches based on saved
  artists. Discover music loads on request and shows results categorized as
  Music by the selected Invidious instance, fixing the unrelated trending
  videos reported in [#33](https://github.com/DVBeckwitt/Videre/issues/33).
- The Muzza-inspired Music player and mini player keep playback, queue, and
  library controls close at hand. The full player adapts to landscape.
- Open **Sound** for speed, pitch, and the Android equalizer. On supported
  devices, adjust individual bands or choose Bass boost or Treble boost.
  Equalizer settings apply to the current listening session.
- Music playback restarts from the beginning when you press Play after a song
  ends, and pending backend retry notices clear when playback completes.

Install **app-release.apk** for the universal build, or choose the APK for
your device. Android 7.0 or newer is required. This release uses version code
60914. `SHA256SUMS` lists the checksums for the release files.
