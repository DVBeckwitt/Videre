# Videre 1.22.33

This update brings customizable phone navigation, seek previews, download folders, and more control over playback and animations.

- **Arrange your navigation.** Open the Home pencil → Navigation to reorder shortcuts, move them between the bottom bar and top icons, or hide them. The default layout is Home, Subscriptions, History, and Search below, with Downloads, Queue, and Playlists above. Search opens with the keyboard ready.
- **Manage your queue.** Open it from the top bar to reorder, remove, or play queued videos. Turn off **Autoplay next in queue** to stop after each video while keeping the queue available. The setting also applies to background audio and downloaded videos.
- **Keep your preferred quality.** Videre remembers your selected resolution across videos and restarts. If that resolution is unavailable, it chooses the closest lower option, or the lowest available resolution. Downloads keep their own quality selection.
- **Choose a download folder.** Select a folder from Settings or Downloads. New downloads use it; existing downloads stay in their current locations. App storage remains the default.
- **Hide watched subscription videos.** Enable the option in Browsing or TV settings to keep watched videos out of your subscription feed. Partly watched videos remain visible, and you can turn the option off at any time.
- **Preview while seeking.** Phone and TV seek controls show a thumbnail and target time when the instance provides storyboard images. The time remains visible when thumbnails are unavailable.
- **Reduce or turn off animations.** Choose Follow system, Reduced, or Off in Appearance settings. Android's Remove animations setting is respected, and playback and notice timing stay intact.
- **Save videos for later.** Continue Watching has been removed from Home. Use Add to playlist → Watch Later, or the player's + button, to save videos. History and remembered playback positions remain available.

Search history is now enabled by default; an existing off preference stays off. Navigation, playback, and animation preferences are included in library backups. Download-folder permissions stay on the device where they were granted.

This release also improves navigation saving, download recovery, watched-feed pagination, and seeking when the queue changes videos. Watching a downloaded video to the end now records its full watched progress, and paused audio playback no longer leaves a loading spinner over the Play button. Shared animation and download handling replaces duplicate code, and unused helpers have been removed.

Install **app-release.apk** for the universal build, or choose the smaller APK matching your device. Android 7.0 or newer is required. Existing Videre installations can update normally. `SHA256SUMS` accompanies the APKs, bundles, and source archives.
