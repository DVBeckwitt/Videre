# Videre 1.22.48 Beta 1

**Local music.** In Music → Library → On this device, choose a folder to play
your own songs with independent speed and pitch controls. Local music is off
until you grant read access through Android’s folder picker. Forgetting the
folder removes its songs from Videre and preserves your files.

**Music downloads.** Videre selects the highest-quality compatible source and
saves MP3 by default. Choose Opus or M4A (AAC) in Settings → YT Music. On
Android 10 and later, new downloads go to Music/Videre by default. You can
export an older private song from its Downloads menu. If a song hasn’t appeared
in Auxio, open Auxio’s Settings → Refresh music. Songs whose titles start with
a period now get a visible filename and keep their original title in the tags.

**Artwork and lyrics.** Music loads artwork through your stock Invidious
instance. Settings → YT Music has separate opt-ins for LRCLIB lyrics and album
covers from MusicBrainz and the Cover Art Archive. Both are off by default and
use your configured network proxy. Opening Lyrics in Now Playing offers to
enable LRCLIB.

**Playback and feeds.** Switch audio quality in Music Now Playing. Watch has a
Repeat button and a combined speed and pitch control. In Your feeds, Continue
watching scrolls away, and adding or removing the shelf preserves your place.

**Install.** Download **app-arm64-v8a-release.apk** for a modern ARM64 phone or
**app-release.apk** for the universal build. This beta requires Android 7.0 or
later and uses Videre’s existing signing key and package. Its version code is
**60947**. See **SHA256SUMS** for the release checksums.
