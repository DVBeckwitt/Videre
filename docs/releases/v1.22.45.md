# Videre 1.22.45

- Playlist details show video count, combined duration, and the update date when available. Incomplete duration information is labeled, and totals support playlists longer than a day.
- Save a private playlist copy to your signed-in Invidious account. Interrupted copies resume against the server's saved entries, including repeated videos.
- Playlist downloads now offer quality and file-format choices. Download video as MP4 or WebM, or choose M4A or WebM audio for audio-only listening.
- Single-video downloads offer the audio languages and subtitle tracks supplied by the instance. Selected subtitles are included in exported videos and available during offline playback. Availability depends on the instance successfully supplying the selected tracks.
- Playlist metadata and saving work in Videos and Music. Short TV screens keep controls and video entries reachable with the remote.
- App-owned network requests retain direct-YouTube blocking across Dart and Android transports, including media, artwork, captions, redirects, and embedded browser requests. Playback and downloads use the selected Invidious instance.

Install **app-release.apk** for the universal build, or choose the APK for your device. Android 7.0 or newer is required. This release uses version code 60915 and the existing Videre signing identity. `SHA256SUMS` lists the checksums for the release files.
