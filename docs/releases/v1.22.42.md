# Videre 1.22.42

- Select and copy video titles and channel names. Copy or share selected text
  from descriptions and comments, and open supported YouTube playlist links
  through your selected Invidious instance. [#32](https://github.com/DVBeckwitt/Videre/issues/32)
- Adjust subtitle size during playback from the More menu on phones and
  tablets or player settings on Android TV. Videre saves your preference.
  [#34](https://github.com/DVBeckwitt/Videre/issues/34)
- The video details page clears the requested backend notice after a
  successful load. [#35](https://github.com/DVBeckwitt/Videre/issues/35)
- The official NerdVPN onion instance can play above 360p when it provides
  compatible adaptive tracks. Available qualities depend on the video and
  instance. Tor bandwidth and latency can cause buffering at 480p or higher;
  choose a lower quality when needed. Playback keeps your selected onion
  connection. [#36](https://github.com/DVBeckwitt/Videre/issues/36)

Install **app-release.apk** for the universal build, or choose the APK for
your device. Android 7.0 or newer is required. This release uses version code
60909. `SHA256SUMS` lists the checksums for the release files.
