# Videre 1.22.47

This release updates the video and Music interfaces, adds local recommendations,
and makes it easier to open and share media links.

- **Video and Music.** Browse video feeds in the Flow-inspired layout and open
  Music from the bottom navigation. Reorder navigation and choose which tabs
  appear on Home. Music adds Quick Picks, Keep Listening, and a player inspired
  by Muzza. Hold a song to play it, play it next, or add it to the queue.
- **Recommendations.** Videre ranks video and Music suggestions on your device.
  Suggestions can learn from playback and feedback, and existing subscriptions
  can help populate video recommendations. Settings let you manage learning.
- **Playback.** Swipe down to minimize the video or Music player. The compact
  watch toolbar keeps Video/Audio, Transcript, and Speed within one tap, with
  Pitch and Repeat in More. Compatible saved media and the playback cache can
  reduce repeated downloads for the same stream.
- **Links.** Open YouTube videos, Shorts, and YouTube Music from another app or
  Android's share menu. To open supported links automatically, enable Videre in
  Android's **Open by default** settings. Videre uses your selected Invidious
  instance for playback. To share from Videre, choose an Invidious or YouTube
  address and optionally include the timestamp.

Install **app-arm64-v8a-release.apk** for most modern Android devices or
**app-release.apk** for the universal build. Android 7.0 or newer is required.
Build **60938** uses the existing Videre package and signing identity.
`SHA256SUMS` lists the release file checksums.
