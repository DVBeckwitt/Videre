# Videre 1.22.48

This release improves Music browsing and playback, adds independent download
folders, and fixes queue playback with the screen off.

- **Music browsing.** Customize the Music layout separately from the main app.
  Search opens in the current section, and pasted Invidious links open through
  your selected instance. Quick Picks snap by column, and Discovery and mixes
  prepare their songs automatically. Back from a song returns to its source.
- **Queue and transitions.** Videre buffers nearby songs. Picking another song
  keeps the current one playing until the replacement is ready. A failed
  selection leaves the current song and queue in place. Under **Settings →
  YT Music → Song transition options**, choose Seamless, Pause between songs,
  Fade out then in, or Crossfade. Android keeps the playback service active
  across automatic transitions and repeats with the screen off.
- **Music player.** Switch between cover art and a matching music video when
  available. Rewind can reuse up to 90 seconds of recently played audio on
  Android. Audio quality changes preserve position and the queue. Synchronized
  lyrics from LRCLIB and covers from MusicBrainz and the Cover Art Archive are
  separate opt-ins.
- **Local audio and downloads.** Play device files from **Music → Library → On
  this device**. Under **Settings → Download folders**, choose separate
  destinations for video and audio. Music supports MP3, Opus, and M4A (AAC).
  On Android 10 and later, the default Music destination is **Music/Videre**.
- **Watch and feeds.** Continue Watching scrolls with the feed. On Android,
  Speed and Pitch share a control, and repeat cycles through off, all, and one.
  Server playlist copy and fullscreen artist navigation include fixes from the
  beta releases.

Install **app-arm64-v8a-release.apk** for most modern Android devices or
**app-release.apk** for the universal build. Android 7.0 or later is required.
Build **60949** uses the existing Videre package and signing key.
**SHA256SUMS** lists the release file checksums.
