# Videre 1.22.48

Music now shows artwork from your selected Invidious instance, including album
images on recognized YouTube Art Tracks. An ordinary upload can use its video
thumbnail. Missing images can fall back to another size from the same instance.

- **YT Music settings.** LRCLIB lyrics and MusicBrainz + Cover Art Archive
  are independent opt-ins, off by default for each profile. Open Lyrics in
  Music Now Playing for synchronized or plain lyrics. With community covers
  enabled, expanded Now Playing looks up the playing song and caches a matched
  cover for reuse across Music. Both providers use your network proxy when
  configured. YouTube requests continue through stock Invidious.
- **Music playback.** Choose Auto or an available audio rendition in Now
  Playing. Changing quality preserves the current position, playback state,
  and queue. Saved audio plays at its downloaded quality.
- **Watch controls.** The speedometer opens Speed and Pitch together on Android.
  The Repeat button cycles through off, all, and one. Volume and fullscreen
  align at the right edge of portrait Watch.

Install **app-arm64-v8a-release.apk** for most modern Android devices or
**app-release.apk** for the universal build. Android 7.0 or later is required.
Build **60944** uses the existing Videre package and signing key.
**SHA256SUMS** lists the release file checksums.
