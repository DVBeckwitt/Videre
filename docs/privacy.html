Videre privacy policy

Videre does not collect user data during normal app use. User accounts are linked to the Invidious instance the user chooses, whether it is self-hosted or operated by a third party. The developers of Videre do not provide public Invidious servers.

Opening Public instances contacts api.invidious.io to load the official directory without sending saved server credentials. Selecting a suggestion fills the address; the chosen server is contacted when you test or use it. Public server operators have their own privacy policies.

Community AI filters are off by default. Enabling a rule downloads the corresponding public list from ailist.flow-tube.org through the configured SOCKS5/Orbot route. Videre sends no search terms, channel or video identifiers, Invidious credentials, or profile settings to the list host. Matches and exceptions are evaluated on the device. Verified lists are shared in a local cache, and rule choices and exceptions belong to the selected profile.

Google Takeout import reads the chosen file on the device. If you choose Load song details in the Music library, Videre sends the video IDs of songs with missing details to your selected Invidious instance through the configured route. It saves the available titles, uploader names, and thumbnail references in your local library.

While Music Home is open, Videre prepares song recommendations through your selected Invidious instance. These requests can include a general music query, names of artists saved to the active profile, genre terms, and video IDs used to verify songs or build a Daily mix. While Video Home is open, Videre can send learned topic queries or a seed video ID to the same instance to prepare its suggestions.

Music browsing, search, channels, and public playlists use your selected Invidious server. Search terms and requested video, channel, and playlist identifiers are sent to that server; Videre does not contact music.youtube.com directly. Google-hosted artwork, including saved Music covers in the player, notifications, and downloads, uses the Invidious image proxy without retrying the original Google URL. These requests follow your configured SOCKS5/Orbot routing policy and use the selected server's access credentials only on that server. Video, audio, and downloads always use your selected Invidious backend's media proxy. Direct playback from YouTube/Google is unavailable, and old settings or imported backups cannot enable it. The app transport blocks official YouTube media, catalog, and artwork destinations, including redirected requests; unsupported artwork is omitted and unavailable proxied media fails without contacting Google directly. The Invidious operator still contacts YouTube upstream to provide content. Saving songs to a local playlist stores them on this device; audio downloads use your chosen download location. Videre does not synchronize a Google-account library.

Playing music from your device is off by default. Choose a folder to grant access to its audio files and subfolders, or give Videre audio permission to use the library indexed by Android. Videre keeps an index of filenames, dates, tags, and artwork on the device. Local tracks stay offline, including when online lyrics or artwork are enabled for YT Music. All Videre profiles on the device use the selected source. Forgetting it clears the local index. You can revoke system audio permission from Android settings. Renaming a file uses the write access required by Android or its document provider.

By default, new Music downloads go into shared Music/Videre on Android 10 and later. Other players with access can read those files. Under Download folders, you can choose private app storage or a folder for audio independently of the video destination.

During Music playback, Videre can request song details and the opening audio for the next and previous queued songs through your selected Invidious instance. Locally available songs use their saved audio.

Opening an online song in the expanded Music player can send its artist and title to your selected Invidious instance to look for a matching music video. Video streaming starts when you select Video.

LRCLIB lyrics are optional and initially disabled for each profile. When you enable them and open Lyrics in Music, Videre sends the song title, artist, and available album and duration to LRCLIB. It uses your configured network proxy when enabled and HTTPS when the proxy is off. LRCLIB receives no Invidious login credentials or video ID. You can disable lyrics lookups in Videre Settings.

MusicBrainz and Cover Art Archive artwork is a separate, initially disabled option under YT Music. While Music Now Playing is open, Videre sends the song title, artist, and available album and duration to MusicBrainz. The Cover Art Archive and Internet Archive receive the matched release or release-group identifier. Requests use the configured network proxy when enabled and HTTPS when the proxy is off. These providers receive no Invidious credentials or YouTube video ID. Matched covers are cached on the device for the active profile.

Bug reports and suggestions are sent only when you choose Send after reviewing the report. The feedback service creates a public issue in the Videre GitHub repository on your behalf; a GitHub account is not required. The title, description, and any attachments you choose become public. Do not include passwords or other private information in your text or screenshot.

Videre interprets supported incoming video, channel, and playlist links as content identifiers and loads them through the selected Invidious server. Sharing offers the selected Invidious address first and an explicit YouTube address option; neither includes saved credentials. Video and Music shares can include a playback timestamp. Constructing a YouTube share address does not make a YouTube network request or change the playback backend. Supported links in descriptions and comments are shown on the selected instance so selecting text does not copy a direct YouTube address.

Embedded sign-in and server-selection browsers also block official YouTube destinations, including redirected resources. Page JavaScript and service-worker networking are disabled to prevent browser protocols from bypassing the protected HTTP route. Sign-in pages that require JavaScript are unsupported. If Android System WebView cannot enforce the required protections, these embedded screens do not load. External links are shown with a Copy action; Videre does not launch another browser or application for them. Opening a copied address yourself uses that other application's networking and privacy settings.

Diagnostics and screenshots are optional and off by default. Diagnostics use a limited list of app and Android versions, device model, playback mode, proxy enabled status, and backend recovery state. They exclude saved login cookies, authorization headers, server addresses, video IDs, watch history, and raw application logs. A selected screenshot is sent to the feedback service and hosted there for display in the public issue, without uploading it to Imgur.

Drafts and recent submission receipts are stored in the app. There is no background submission. The feedback service stores submission records to prevent duplicate issues and uses short-lived, hashed network-address information and request limits to reduce spam. As with any network service, its host receives connection information; app feedback follows the configured SOCKS5/Orbot routing policy. Public GitHub issues and their attachments may be copied or cached by others.

Videre developers do not own, collect, or share user data outside user-initiated feedback.
